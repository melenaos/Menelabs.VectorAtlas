Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

General policy

If you're not satisfied with your Indie or Business license purchase, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date, no questions asked. After 14 days, purchases are final.

How to request a refund

Email info@menelabs.com with your order number or the email address used at checkout. Refunds are typically processed within a few business days.

What happens to your license

Once a refund is issued, the commercial (attribution-removal) license is considered void. You can, of course, still use the Asset for free under CC BY 4.0, the same as any other user.

Payment processor

Paddle, as Merchant of Record, processes the actual refund once approved. See Paddle's Buyer Terms for how refunds are executed on their end and typical timing for the funds to appear back on your payment method.

Contact

Refund questions: info@menelabs.com.
